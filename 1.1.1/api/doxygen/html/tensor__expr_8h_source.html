27/* =========================================================================
28 * Lazy Tensor Expression Engine + Operator Fusion Planner
29 *
30 * A deferred-execution IR: tensor operations build a DAG of ExprNodes
31 * instead of materializing results immediately. The fusion planner
32 * groups eligible elementwise/reduction ops into fused kernels and
33 * schedules matmuls/GEMMs on the SIMD/CPU backend, enabling torch.compile
34 * style "graph capture -> fusion -> execute" semantics.
35 * ========================================================================= */
36
